## Goal
Build the transport adapter family under adapters/transport/: the subagent kind (TR-1), the headless kind under an operating-system job (TR-2) and the multiplexer kind (TR-3) with its herdr recipe adapters/transport/multiplexer/herdr.md, plus the machine probe scripts/team/probe.py that resolves the best available kind in its own shell (file names: plan decision D-3). The same change documents the family in ADAPTERS.md, adds the opt-in multiplexer row to the setup register skills/afk/setup/MANIFEST.md, and records the herdr probe rows in providers/CONFORMANCE.md. The slice ends at the kinds and the probe: the team entry script's checks, names and charter files, and the desktop-tool kind (TR-4) are other subtasks.
