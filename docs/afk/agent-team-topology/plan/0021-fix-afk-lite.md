## Goal
Make /afk:fix run the afk-lite pattern (the small built-in team) through the contact agent. In an interactive run afk-lite stops for the human after its design step, on a one-way door and on a DISAGREE-FINAL point; in driven mode it classifies its design per the decision protocol and stops only on a one-way door or a DISAGREE-FINAL point, ending with the new status needs_decision. The builder writes code and tests and leaves the commit to the caller. The five callers in /afk:execute and /afk:preflight gain the route for the design stop and for needs_decision, and a test pins that route. The slice ends at /afk:fix and its callers; the /afk:bug fixer's own design stop is another subtask.

## Conflict procedure
If a binding decision in SDD/ADR is wrong / infeasible / contradicts reality
during implementation, classify per the decision protocol (`DECISIONS.md`,
workflow plugin root): a two-way-door correction is recorded in
`plan/DECISIONS.md` and implemented; a one-way door or a tie exits
`design_conflict` quoting the SDD section + the conflict. Never override off
the record. Parked conflicts route back to `/afk:grill-solution` for a
superseding ADR.
