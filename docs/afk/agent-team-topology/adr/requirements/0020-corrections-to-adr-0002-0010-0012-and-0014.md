# Corrections to ADR-0002, ADR-0010, ADR-0012 and ADR-0014

> Status: Decided by agent
> Audited: not yet
> Layer: Requirements
> Context ticket: agent-team-topology

Four earlier records disagree with records or settled rows written after them. Their bodies stay as written; this record supersedes the statements below in part and takes no decision of its own.

ADR-0010 says only the agent in the builder role commits and pushes, and that roles other than the builder cannot publish. The PRD's access row "Commit and push" also lets the human and the contact agent commit and push, and under `/afk:autopilot` the contact agent pushes the feature branch before the first subtask starts (grill log row of 2026-10-05 on the design audit, L20). Among spawned agents, two stated exceptions hold: afk-lite under `/afk:fix` leaves the commit to the caller (ADR-0014), and the `/afk:bug` fixer commits, pushes and opens 1 Draft change request, only on its own fix branch (ADR-0017, AC-010, AC-060). Under `/afk:autopilot` the subtask agent is the builder (solution grill S-227), so it is no exception. ADR-0014 calls itself the one exception to ADR-0010; it is one of these 2.

ADR-0012 says a step may use another pattern. Includes belong to the pattern, and a step lists only the roles it starts (solution grill S-228); the pattern that includes owns the link (solution grill S-229). The included patterns' steps follow the including pattern's own steps, in the order of its includes (grill log row of 2026-10-05, Q28, decided by the agent; PRD AC-033).

ADR-0002 is superseded in full by ADR-0018, which says so. ADR-0002's status line now reads Superseded by ADR-0018 (grill log row of 2026-10-05 on audit round 2, decided by the agent); its body stays as written. Nothing in the knowledge store is promoted before it is deleted (solution grill S-87).
