# Consultation is advisory: no gate verdict changes, no human decision replaced

> Status: Decided by agent
> Audited: not yet
> Layer: Requirements
> Context ticket: research-enhancement

A consultation report informs a grill, review, adversary or diagnosis; it never changes a review finding class, a review or adversary verdict, or a human decision (PRD AC-018). Dispositions are `supported`, `qualified`, `unresolved`; votes are metadata; a factual dispute is checked against an official source or a local probe before it reaches a human, and a dispute with no fresh judge is preserved as `unresolved` (AC-016, AC-017). Trade-off accepted: a strong multi-provider consensus still cannot close a gate on its own. Source: `MERGED-PLAN.md` §1 A4 (:26), A7 (:29), §2 divergence D2 (:49).
